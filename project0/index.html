Part 1: Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

What changes across the series?

I took the first portrait from very close range, then stepped farther away while increasing the focal length to keep my face at roughly the same size in each frame. At 12 mm, my nose is much closer to the camera than my ears and the sides of my face, so it projects disproportionately large and creates the familiar distorted selfie look. As the camera moves back, those depth differences become small relative to the total camera-to-subject distance, and my facial proportions appear more natural. The longer focal length restores similar framing; the change in perspective can come from the greater camera distance.
